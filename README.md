> Abiogenesis is the natural process by which life arises from non-living matter, such as simple organic compounds. It occurs when prebiotic chemical reactions assemble increasingly complex polymers, resulting in self-sustaining molecular systems capable of replication and metabolism. The process of abiogenesis marked the transition from chemistry to biology, ultimately giving rise to the common ancestor of all life on Earth.

## RNA World

### RNA Self-Replication

- **[A small polymerase ribozyme that can synthesize itself and its complementary strand](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7618777/)** - 2026. Gianni, et al. A study describing a small polymerase ribozyme capable of synthesizing both itself and its complementary strand, providing insights into the potential mechanisms of RNA self-replication in the early RNA world.

- **[Trinucleotide substrates under pH–freeze–thaw cycles enable open-ended exponential RNA replication by a polymerase ribozyme](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12226338/)** - 2025. Attwater, et al. A study demonstrating that trinucleotide substrates under pH–freeze–thaw cycles can facilitate open-ended exponential RNA replication by a polymerase ribozyme, providing insights into potential mechanisms of RNA self-replication in the early RNA world.

- **[A minimal model of stable autocatalytic RNA-replication through integration of replication, molecular regulation, and energy conversion](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.25.740712v1/)** - 2026. Conrad, et al. A study presenting a minimal model for stable autocatalytic RNA replication that integrates replication, molecular regulation, and energy conversion, providing insights into the potential dynamics of early RNA-based life.

- **[RNA ligase ribozymes with a small catalytic core](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10219994/)** - 2023. Nomura, Yokobayashi. A study describing RNA ligase ribozymes that possess a small catalytic core, providing insights into the minimal structural requirements for RNA-catalyzed ligation reactions.

### Prebiotic synthesis of RNA

The RNA world hypothesis suggests that early life forms may have relied on RNA both to store genetic information and to catalyze chemical reactions. This concept is central to understanding the transition from simple molecules to complex, self-replicating systems in the origin of life.

- **[Setting the geological scene for the origin of life and continuing open questions about its emergence](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7615569/)** - 2024. Westall, Brack, Fairén, Schulte et al. A comprehensive review that details the geochemical conditions, rock lithologies, and environmental timescales of the Hadean Earth to establish realistic boundary constraints for prebiotic chemistry.

- **[Catalytic Synthesis of Polyribonucleic Acid on Prebiotic Rock Glasses](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1089/ast.2022.0027/)** - 2022. Jerome, et al. A study demonstrating that prebiotic rock glasses can catalyze the formation of polyribonucleic acids, providing insights into potential pathways for the emergence of RNA-based life.

- **[Influence of Silicate Rock Glass Compositions on the Efficacy of Prebiotic RNA Polymerization Reactions: The Case of 3’,5’ Cyclic Guanosine Monophosphate](https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/syst.202300016/)** - 2023. Spooner, et al.A study investigating how different silicate rock glass compositions affect the efficiency of prebiotic RNA polymerization reactions, highlighting the role of mineral surfaces in the potential emergence of RNA-based life.

- **[Interstep compatibility of a model for the prebiotic synthesis of RNA consistent with Hadean natural history](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2516418122/)** - 2025. Hirikawa, et al. A study examining the compatibility of different steps in a model for prebiotic RNA synthesis, ensuring consistency with the known conditions of the Hadean Earth.

### Prebiotic synthesis of ribonucleoside triphosphates (rNTPs)

- **[Prebiotic chemistry: a review of nucleoside phosphorylation and polymerization](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9832566/)** - 2023. Guo, et al. A review summarizing the current understanding of nucleoside phosphorylation and polymerization under prebiotic conditions, highlighting potential pathways for the formation of ribonucleoside triphosphates.

- **[Phosphorylation, Oligomerization and Self-assembly in Water Under Potential Prebiotic Conditions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6295206/)** - 2018. Gibard, et al. A study exploring the phosphorylation, oligomerization, and self-assembly of nucleotides in water under conditions that could have been present on the prebiotic Earth, providing insights into the potential formation of ribonucleoside triphosphates.

- **[Prebiotic Synthesis of ATP: A Terrestrial Volcanism-Dependent Pathway](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-1729/13/3/731)** - 2023. Chu, Zhang. A study proposing a pathway for the prebiotic synthesis of ATP that is dependent on terrestrial volcanism, providing insights into the potential availability of ribonucleoside triphosphates on the early Earth.
